Privacy Policy for What Are The Odds

What Are The Odds does not collect, store, or share personal information, and does not use user accounts, tracking, or unique identifiers.

The app may use Google Play services to collect anonymous crash reports and basic usage metrics to improve stability and performance.

The app may contain links to external websites (such as social media). Any information shared on those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies.
